A valid historical CC0 grant must not be casually assumed revoked, but the mirror's claim alone does not prove that grant for this exact copy. **Hold final adoption; no public raw clip library or game payload derived from it was added.** Obfuscation/compression is not license clearance.

### 5.2 Useful estimates without a false FPS promise

The [reproducible model](evidence/a1-final/performance-risk-model.json) uses the measured construction/vegetation counts:

- **Illustrative subset only:** two 20K-triangle shade trees + three 13,872-triangle shrubs + the 17,588-triangle construction study = **99,204 triangles**, **37 uninstanced base geometry batches**. Omits hero, flowering tree, new finishing geometry/PBR, surroundings and extra passes. Not a scene benchmark or final G1 estimate.
- The same rough layout using publisher-listed ~312K Jacaranda × 2 and ~52K Shrub 02 × 3 plus the study is **~797,588 triangles**, before those omissions. Those vegetation numbers are publisher metadata, **not acquired-package measurements**. Instancing can reduce submissions/storage, not magically remove per-visible-instance vertex/shadow work.
- A 1024² RGBA8 texture with a full mip chain is approximately **5.33 MiB** uncompressed; 2048² **21.33 MiB**; 4096² **85.33 MiB**. A three-map set at 2K is ~64 MiB; ten such sets ~640 MiB before overhead. Actual normal/scalar channel formats may differ. File PNG/JPEG/meshopt size is not residency.
- Idealized 8-bits-per-texel block-compressed full chains would be ~1.33 / 5.33 / 21.33 MiB respectively. **KTX2 tooling/format support and final textures are not proven**, so do not bank those savings. Decode/upload peaks, CPU copies, duplicated images, render targets and buffers add pressure.
- At 1080p/55° vFOV, an idealized 8 m tree at 20 m is ~415 px tall, at 40 m ~207 px. Aggressive thinning can remain clearly visible. Do not apply a triangle-only LOD threshold; use projected coverage and silhouette checks.
- 60 FPS corresponds to 16.67 ms/frame; 30 FPS to 33.33 ms. These are reciprocal definitions, **not achieved rates**. No conversion from triangles/SwiftShader seconds to Victus FPS is valid.
