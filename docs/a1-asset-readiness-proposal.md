## 1. Executive summary

The most credible route is **original concept-specific architecture and character finishing, combined with a small inspected CC0 material/vegetation/motion set**, through editable Blender masters and validated glTF exports into Babylon.js. An unrelated free city pack would not establish the landmark's identity or the intended character quality.

## 3. G1 requirements and proposed defaults

G1 is a small, real, concept-aligned browser slice, not a fake HUD over a background render. Final dimensions, camera framing and numeric tolerances must be recorded before production. These proposed defaults make planning concrete without pretending that the one concept image supplies a floor plan.

- **One landmark:** complete walk-around exterior, recognizable curved blue-neutral glazing, horizontal architectural bands, structural depth, recessed/layered ground floor and entrance, roof transitions, plausible sides/rear and restrained blue signage/light accents. No explorable interior required.
- **One compact plaza:** constructed pale-gray paving, joint scale, credible walking routes and curb/road edges. A short pedestrian-to-road connection proves transitions, scale and material continuity. Keep camera-visible boundaries intentionally designed, not an empty infinite plane.
- **One fixed male hero:** original identity inspired by the concept's silhouette/outfit, not a copy of a proprietary game character. Dark tousled hair, sunglasses, dark jacket with authored blue accents, white shirt, cargo trousers and blue/white sneakers. One fixed body and outfit; no slider UI.
- **Limited movement:** idle, walk, run and basic blends/start-stop/turn behavior; third-person camera and bounded traversal sufficient to inspect the character, plaza and landmark. No climbing, combat, parkour or full gameplay controller promised here.
- **Small landscape/furniture family:** a few urban shade trees, one flowering accent solution, shrubs, planters, benches and lights. Instance a limited coherent set, not dozens of unrelated packs.
- **Daylight first:** readable surfaces, believable sun/sky relationship, grounded shadows and controlled blue accents. Daylight must work without excessive bloom, darkness, depth-of-field or wet-road reflections hiding defects.

**Delivery architecture remains Windows Chromium, keyboard/mouse, React + strict TypeScript + Babylon.js.** WebGPU-first, explicit WebGL2 compatibility path; forced backends never silently switch. React owns application UI/state presentation, not Babylon's frame loop or scene objects. DCC software is an offline authoring tool: the player will not need Blender, MPFB or a desktop game engine.

The master plan's provisional envelopes remain **unmeasured, unapproved proposals**, not new binding budgets: native 1080p/render scale 1, 60 FPS aspiration, roughly 0.8–1.5M visible triangles and ~250 submissions including shadows, 60 MiB critical/100 MiB total transfer. Use the master plan's warm-up, repeated runs and agreed sustained run. These figures are not proof that the target fits an unknown HP Victus configuration.

### 4.2 Landmark — original concept-specific architecture

**Route:** original editable Blender master, modular where repetition is real, unique where identity demands it. Free material components may help; no researched free building replaces this brief. The retained Poly Haven apartment/factory candidates belong to later specialist families, not this landmark.

| Production unit               | Required original work / export implication                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Overall shell and proportions | Resolve plan, height, floor rhythm, curvature, setbacks and plaza relationship. Keep full exterior volume, not a front-only stage set.                                               |
| Curved facade system          | Author repeatable curved bay modules with controlled chord/tessellation and consistent pivots. Prove smooth highlights at the nearest permitted camera before multiplying bays.      |
| Glazing bays/mullions         | Separate blue-neutral glass from metal and opaque spandrels; model visible frame thickness/recesses. Avoid making every pane a unique transparent draw call.                         |
| Horizontal bands              | Model projecting bands, underside depth, corner/curve transitions and readable floor separation. Do not replace identity-bearing geometry with painted stripes.                      |
| Ground floor and entrance     | Layered canopy/columns, recessed doors, mullion rhythm, plinths and entrance transition. Visibility into glazing may use bounded authored depth, not an implied explorable interior. |
| Sides and rear                | Continue structural logic, service access, joints and glazing treatment; no raw backside or blank wall hidden by one screenshot.                                                     |
| Roof and transitions          | Parapet/coping, roofline, termination details and plausible setbacks visible from orbit/high camera limits. No unnecessary detailed rooftop equipment unseen in G1.                  |
| Signage and blue accents      | Original CodeVerse signage/graphics with confirmed font/logo rights; geometry/emissive treatment readable in daylight, not neon overexposure. Elaborate hologram is optional.        |
| Collision                     | Separate simple walkable/blocking shapes, doorway treatment and clearance. Do not collide against every mullion or pane.                                                             |
| LOD/export assemblies         | Preserve curved silhouette, band rhythm, entrance depth and material identity; reduce hidden structure and distant small details first. Bake modifiers only on export copies.        |

**Risks:** a single perspective leaves sides/rear/dimensions unknown; dense unique facade meshes increase submissions; transmission/reflections can be costly; unbaked procedural materials fail export; naive decimation breaks curves. Use one representative curved bay plus entrance junction as the first future production-path proof. A rough construction study may be useful internally, but it is never the final visual pass. Reject cylinders/cubes with a facade texture, flat image cards, screenshot-to-3D reconstructions and fake background replacements.

### 4.3 Plaza and short road link — constructed place, not a plane

**Essential original work:** compact site plan and bounded walk route; modular paving slabs/fields with credible joints and trims; road/sidewalk separation, curbs, corners/end pieces, material transitions and entrance landing. Include a short route connecting the plaza to the street edge. Model steps only if the site needs a level change and provide a credible accessible ramp/continuous route rather than decorative stairs blocking access. Numeric dimensions/clearances must be documented; this proposal does not certify statutory accessibility compliance.

Provide planter bases, bench pads, light mounting, small drain/utility-cover details where visible, and intentional edge termination. Use actual relief for camera-visible curb/step edges; use normal/roughness detail rather than geometry for every microscopic groove. Keep road camber/grade and drainage visually plausible without building a road network.

**Postpone:** junction libraries, traffic lights, lane AI, highway tools, terrain generator, parking systems, full drainage engineering, wetness simulation, broad decal libraries. Only include a marking/crossing if the short route actually uses it. No Roadscape or paid dependency. Original mesh modules are the ₹0 route, not a claim that original labor is free.

### 4.4 Fixed hero — generated base plus substantial original character work

**Base candidate:** MPFB/MakeHuman core assets; use only individually cleared core CC0 resources and original additions. Generator capability is a publisher claim, not proof of a finished photoreal or concept-quality protagonist. The extension publisher itself distinguishes parametric creation from a catalogue of scanned finished people.

1. Define one fixed body, height, face identity and outfit silhouette. Start with a compatible stable Blender/MPFB pair, pin versions, record generator settings and source assets. Avoid unrelated community packs by default.
2. Refine anatomy/proportions/face/ears/neck, eye fit, skin values and visible hands. Fingers need usable topology and weights even without hand-interaction gameplay. Do not hide a poor face behind sunglasses.
3. Create concept-aligned tousled hair, brows and hairline. Choose a bounded card/mesh solution with tested alpha/sorting and silhouette; Blender particle/curve hair is not assumed portable.
4. Originally author jacket thickness/collar/cuffs/zip/seams, blue graphics, shirt, cargo pocket forms/straps, trousers and branded-free sneaker geometry/sole/laces. Fabric weave belongs primarily in textures; do not model every stitch. Fit the fixed pose/body before generalizing.
5. Choose one canonical humanoid **deform skeleton**, joint naming/map, meter scale, root/pelvis hierarchy and documented A- or T-rest pose. Keep control rig separate; bake constraints/IK to export bones. Test bind/rest pose before retargeting.
6. Fit skin/clothes together; remove hidden body surfaces only on export copy or use documented masks. Check shoulders, elbows, wrists, crotch, knees and ankles; repair weights and add necessary correctives. No broad morph/customization claim follows from one fixed fit.
7. Inspect the free animation subset for idle/walk/run and in-place/root-motion variants. Prefer offline baked retargeting for repeatability. Map reference poses, root motion, bone lengths and ground height; set controller velocity to clip stride. Babylon's retargeting documentation describes capabilities, not a verified result on this skeleton/version.
8. If the free tier lacks suitable clips, author the missing locomotion originally or hold the path. Budget includes retarget cleanup/small corrections, not a guaranteed full replacement animation library. Define idle↔walk↔run, stopping/turning and foot contact at multiple speeds. No slide, ground penetration, shoulder collapse or garment clipping accepted because the pack is free.
9. UV/bake normalized skin/cloth/shoe/hair maps; bound material slots, bones/influences and texture sets after measurements. Keep editable source and a separate export duplicate. Export selected skin/deform bones and clips to glTF; validate and reimport before future Babylon inspection.

**Main uncertainty:** no base mesh, source rig, garment, clip or export has been inspected. Rigged ≠ animated; listed GLB ≠ compatible deformation. Broad NPCs/customization come later. **Human Generator remains excluded under ₹0**, irrespective of general permission to proceed.

### 4.5 Vegetation — curated urban planting

**Proposed minimum:** 2–3 shade-tree instances from one approved species/family with modest original variation, one flowering accent tree/solution, one shrub family and integrated original planters. Limited groundcover is optional, not a lawn/forest.

- **Jacaranda Tree, Poly Haven:** publisher reports **312K tris**, **24.2 m width**, LOD/Geometry Nodes tags; broad green canopy preview. Useful branching/canopy candidate, not a verified flowering accent or browser-ready street tree. Three unmodified instances would already be roughly 936K source triangles before other content; instancing reduces some CPU/draw overhead, not per-instance visible vertex/fragment work.
- **Shrub 02, Poly Haven:** publisher reports **52K tris**, **5.3 m width**, LOD tag and fine-leaf foliage. Inspect suitability for clipped/coherent urban beds; arbitrary shrinking may make leaves unnaturally tiny. Neither tag proves useful runtime LODs.
- **Flowering accent:** no selected ready-made candidate. Preferred fallback is original flowering canopy/card/atlas authoring on a cleared/original branch structure, with authored pink bloom distribution and natural branch logic—not just tinting every leaf pink. Keep this **essential but unresolved** until a representative source/production path is credible.

Inspect alpha coverage, double-sided surfaces, geometry density, material slots, UV islands, map resolution and any procedural dependencies. Author silhouette-preserving LODs, reduce internal leaf layers, pack atlases, use alpha testing where visually suitable, and measure overdraw/shadow cost from plaza camera and backlight. Trunk collision only; no leaf collision. Choose instance variation carefully and ensure no repeated identical top silhouette dominates. Optional wind must use bounded Babylon-compatible deformation with stable roots/bounds and a deliberate shadow policy; no Blender wind modifier portability assumed. Wind is not a readiness blocker for static G1 planting.

### 4.6 Props — one coherent civic family

**Original essentials:** one bench design, one plaza light design, one planter family, and a restrained signage/mounting family. Simple underlying geometry is acceptable; visibly crude proportions, razor edges, missing mounts, toy-scale surfaces and inconsistent materials are not.

Model believable seat/slat thickness, metal supports/fasteners where visible, concrete planter coping/soil seating, pole bases and joints. Share trims/materials and instance repeated items. Do not build a giant library or give every bolt unique materials.

**Optional:** one bin, a short safety barrier/bollard family if required by the site, and one utility cabinet. Exact free cabinet candidate below is weathered and may clash with a clean civic plaza; it is not a mandatory download. Background clutter is not a substitute for careful primary assets.

### 4.7 Materials and lighting — portable PBR, no engine magic

| Surface family                 | Proposed path and main check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Glass                          | Original metal/roughness-compatible glazing setup, controlled tint/roughness and reflection strategy. Compare opaque reflective glazing versus limited transmission; check sorting/interior visibility and both backends. No promise of ray-traced city reflections. |
| Concrete / pale stone / paving | Review Concrete034 for appropriate smooth concrete use; author pale-gray plaza slabs/joints originally. Concrete/plaster preview is not automatically correct paving. Reject mossy PavingStones036 as the main clean plaza finish.                                   |
| Asphalt                        | Inspect Road012A as a small material candidate; its old/worn appearance may need controlled adjustment or original asphalt instead. No baked-in markings adopted without checking tiling/scale.                                                                      |
| Metal / wood                   | Original normalized civic-furniture materials; roughness/edge treatment and grain scale matter more than high-resolution noise. Clear any acquired maps separately.                                                                                                  |
| Skin / fabric / rubber         | Core cleared skin where suitable, substantial original finish and original cloth/blue graphics/shoe-rubber materials. No procedural subsurface/hair shader automatically survives export.                                                                            |
| Bark / leaves / flowers        | Cleared source maps plus original atlas/flower work; alpha, mip fringes and backlit readability require target-camera tests.                                                                                                                                         |

Normalize to glTF metallic-roughness PBR: base color/emissive in the appropriate color space, scalar/normal/occlusion data treated as data; use OpenGL normal orientation, verify tangent basis, and pack channels deliberately. Do not double-apply AO or import DirectX normal maps unchanged. Bake unsupported procedural layers and sculpted microdetail to export textures. Core glTF does not carry arbitrary Blender shader graphs or geometric displacement; retain silhouette geometry where needed.

**Texture working defaults, not accepted budgets:** start most reusable environment sets at 1–2K; allow a justified higher-resolution hero/unique surface only if the actual camera needs it. Use consistent texel density and shared trims/atlases. Raw texture ZIP size is not network payload or GPU residency. A single uncompressed 2048² RGBA8 texture is 16 MiB before mipmaps (~21.3 MiB with a full chain); several maps and copies add up quickly. Track runtime dimensions, formats, mip levels, residency and decoded/transcoded cost, not only GLB bytes.

KTX2/Basis is a future validated encoding option, not a magic lossless switch. Compare ETC1S versus UASTC for color, normals and alpha foliage; inspect block artifacts and backend transcode support. Retain lossless/source textures, generate exports reproducibly and self-host required decoder/transcoder files. Do not combine geometry compressors or extensions without verifying the actual loader path.

**Lighting plan:** one deliberately framed sun/sky daylight setup, calibrated exposure/tonemapping, bounded environment lighting and justified reflection solution; curated shadow casters/receivers, shadow distance/resolution/bias and plant shadow LOD. Preserve skin/fabric color and blue accent detail. No Unreal Lumen/Nanite/Blueprint dependence; no assumption a cinematic clip's lighting is attainable unchanged in a browser. A custom environment map must be originally authored or separately cleared before acquisition. Day/night cycle, extensive dynamic lights and weather are deferred.

### 7.2 Asset pipeline contract

1. **Brief and register:** freeze one bounded asset brief/camera requirement; save source/license evidence and accept/hold/reject page review. Original assets get provenance/contribution records too.
2. **Acquire or author:** obtain only the selected cleared files or make the original study. Keep raw sources immutable and outside runtime/public directories; no bulk-library downloading.
3. **Editable master:** organize named collections/meshes/materials/actions, real-world meters, pivots/module grid and source textures. Record scripts/settings/version. Preserve non-destructive originals.
4. **Cleanup:** remove helpers/unneeded geometry on a duplicate; resolve normals/tangents, nonmanifold errors where relevant, transforms, hierarchy and source dependencies. Inspect actual counts, not just publisher labels.
5. **UV and bake:** density targets by nearest camera; seams/overlap/lightmap needs documented; bake unsupported shaders/details and check mip padding. Avoid baking lighting twice.
6. **Material normalization:** shared PBR families, color/data-space rules, normal orientation, alpha policy and texture sizes. Resolve unsupported extensions before export.
7. **Collision:** separate simple named colliders and walkable surfaces; include curb/ramp/entry clearance and bounded traversal tests. Never auto-use render meshes indiscriminately.
8. **LOD and optimization:** preserve identity/silhouette first; deduplicate, trim material slots, instance suitable statics, prune leaf interiors/hidden surfaces, simplify skeleton only where validated. LOD selection/storage is an explicit Babylon/manifest task, not assumed automatic from a glTF tag.
9. **Export:** selected runtime objects only to glTF 2.0/GLB, baked transforms/actions/deform bones as appropriate; document axes/handedness conversion, meter scale, animation names and exact exporter preset. Retain editable source separately.
10. **Static validation/reimport:** verify buffers/images/extension requirements, zero glTF errors, reviewed warnings, sane bounds/pivots/scale, texture presence and pose/clip integrity. Reimport/compare with master and repeat export from clean source.
11. **Babylon integration:** later load through a version-matched loader; self-host decoder/transcoder assets rather than accidental CDN dependency; explicit base paths and cancellable loads. Own/dispose meshes, skeletons, animation groups, materials and shared textures safely; preserve strict React/Babylon separation.
12. **Visual/performance review:** actual browser cameras, landmark walk-around, material closeups, foliage backlight, hero pose/joint/locomotion tests, both backends, repeated loads/unloads and representative hardware runs. Report draw/submission count including shadows, geometry, texture residency/transfer, frame pacing and context/resource behavior.
13. **Accept/rework/reject:** attach evidence and reviewer decision. Regenerate the same deliverable using recorded source/settings; do not accept a one-off lucky export. Failed rights/source/visual/performance criteria remain blockers.

**Character additions:** base/settings record → canonical rest/bind pose → original fitting/hair/garments → weight/corrective tests → clip rights/retarget map → baked locomotion and transitions → skinned export/reimport → Babylon deform/contact tests. [MPFB export guidance](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/exporting/index.html) suggests export copies/helper removal and GameEngine materials/rig, with an FBX illustration; this is **not proof of our GLB workflow**. glTF/Babylon axis/root conversion must be checked, not patched by unexplained scales.
